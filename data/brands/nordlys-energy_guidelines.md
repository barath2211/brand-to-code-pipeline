# Nordlys Energy brand guidelines (excerpt)

Our identity is calm, northern and trustworthy.

## Colour
- Primary colour is Fjord Blue #12355b. Use it for headers and buttons.
- Secondary accent is Aurora Green #2bb673, used sparingly for tags.
- Page background is white #FFFFFF with light panels in Snow #f4f6f8.
- Body text is Graphite #222831; supporting text is Slate #5f6b7a.

## Typography
- Headings use "Source Serif Pro".
- Body copy uses "Source Sans Pro" at 16px.

## Shape
- Corners are softly rounded, 6px.
